Star-Pattern Torque Challenge

This is the manual's own Step 19 photo. Bolt 1 is labeled for you so there's one correct starting point — the rest are masked. Tap 2 through 8 in the correct star-crossing order, the same pattern used for spring plate and stuffing box bolts. Tapping in reading order (2,3,4,5…) around the ring seats the gasket unevenly.

Bolt 1 is marked for you — tap it to start, then find 2 through 8.

Equal-Gap / Even-Protrusion Check

Tests manual steps 5 (holder gaps) and 8 (shaft O-Ring protrusion): can you tell when two sides are already equal, and when they're not \u2014 by eye, the way you'd actually check it? No numbers on screen. Diagnose first; you only get to adjust if adjustment is actually needed.
